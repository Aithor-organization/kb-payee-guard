RECITALS

WHEREAS, BMTI and Luitpold have entered into that certain Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of December 14, 2007 (the “Purchase Agreement”) pursuant to which BMTI has agreed to sell, and Luitpold has agreed to purchase, all of the assets that are primarily related to the Business (other than the Excluded Assets);

The first six months of each Forecast shall be binding upon Luitpold (the “Binding Requirements”) and may not be changed without BMTI’s prior written consent.
