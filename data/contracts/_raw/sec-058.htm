RECITALS

 

  A. Astellas and NGX have entered into a Distribution, Marketing and License Agreement, dated 18th June, 2009 (the “Agreement”), and in which NGX has granted Astellas certain rights to use, promote, market, sell, offer for sale, import, export or otherwise commercialize the Existing Product in the Territory.

 

  B.
